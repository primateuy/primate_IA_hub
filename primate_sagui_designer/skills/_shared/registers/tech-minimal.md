# Register: tech-minimal

Composition references (live capture, composition only): linear.app, vercel.com, raycast.com,
cal.com. Do NOT take their dark backgrounds, their accent colors or their type as defaults.

## Traits
- **Hero:** one statement headline in display size (2 lines max), one short lead, ONE primary
  CTA. Optional secondary as a text link, never a second button of equal weight. Below or beside
  it: the product itself (real screenshot, real interface fragment, real object photo) — never a
  stat row, never three icon cards.
- **Rhythm:** sections separated by generous space (spacing scale top step), each opened by a
  small uppercase eyebrow → h2 (2–6 words) → one-line lead. Alternation is by *density* and
  *image presence*, not necessarily by light/dark; one deliberate contrast section is enough.
- **Density:** low. Max 3 columns; a lot of empty space is the point. Body text width ≤ 65ch.
- **Structure devices:** thin rules or spacing, not boxes. Cards only for things that are cards
  (plans, products, integrations). Borders 1px at low contrast, radius small and consistent.
- **Imagery:** the product or the real work. If there is nothing to show, use typographic
  composition (large numbers with real meaning, a list set as display type) rather than stock.
- **Type:** neutral, precise sans for body; display can be the same family in a heavier or
  tighter cut, or a contrasting grotesque. Tracking slightly negative on display sizes.
- **Motion budget:** hover states (150–250 ms, easing), one entrance sequence for the hero only.
  No per-section fades. CSS only.
- **Nav:** 3–5 items, logo left, single CTA right, compact height, subtle bottom rule or none.
- **Footer:** 3–4 columns of real links, small type, muted; a dark footer is optional, not the
  default.

## What ruins this register
Uneven section spacing; mixing 3+ font weights; more than one accent; gradients; decorative
icons; testimonials with stock avatars; a hero with two equal buttons and a stats row.

## Verifier notes (add to rubric C when this register is set)
- C5 Hero has exactly one primary action and shows the real product/work
- C6 Section spacing is uniform (same scale step between all top-level sections)
