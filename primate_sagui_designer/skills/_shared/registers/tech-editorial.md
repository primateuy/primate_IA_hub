# Register: tech-editorial

Composition references (live capture, composition only): stripe.com, anthropic.com, figma.com.
Do NOT take Stripe's gradient, Anthropic's palette or any of their type as defaults.

## Traits
- **Hero:** very large display type, often full-width, sometimes over a full-bleed brand block or
  image; the headline is an idea, not a feature. One CTA plus an optional "read more" link into
  content. May start dark and open into light.
- **Rhythm:** strong, deliberate light/dark alternation; long sections with room to breathe;
  content sections (articles, case studies, research, docs) are first-class citizens on the
  homepage, not a footer afterthought.
- **Density:** variable by design — a sparse hero, then a denser grid of content cards, then a
  sparse statement section. The contrast in density is part of the composition.
- **Structure devices:** big section numbers or dates only when they carry meaning; wide rules;
  a visible grid; pull-quotes from real content.
- **Imagery:** brand-owned graphics (diagrams, abstract compositions derived from the product),
  product screenshots framed with intent, photography with a consistent treatment. Motion in the
  hero graphic is acceptable if it is the signature.
- **Type:** an expressive display face (serif or wide grotesque) at large scale, paired with a
  neutral body; a utility/mono face for labels, code or data.
- **Motion budget:** one signature moment (hero graphic, scroll-linked reveal of a diagram) and
  hover; everything else static. CSS only in v1 — if the signature needs JS, say so and reduce it.
- **Nav:** can be richer (dropdowns for products/resources), still restrained; a top-level CTA.
- **Footer:** large, structured, dark or contrast-colored; sitemap-style columns.

## What ruins this register
A brand gradient with no brand behind it; broadsheet hairlines everywhere; density without
content to justify it; big type with weak copy.

## Verifier notes
- C5 Display type is paired with real, specific copy (an idea, not a slogan)
- C6 Dark/light alternation is deliberate and each section has enough content for its size
