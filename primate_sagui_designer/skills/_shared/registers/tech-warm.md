# Register: tech-warm

Composition references (live capture, composition only): basecamp.com, 37signals.com,
mercury.com, lightbulbmoment.com. Do NOT take their palettes or illustration style as defaults.

## Traits
- **Hero:** a headline written like a person talking (a claim, a promise, a contradiction),
  display size with character; a short paragraph that says what the company actually does;
  one CTA in plain words ("Pedí una demo", "Hablemos"). Image: real people, real place, or an
  illustration in the brand's own hand — never stock, never a floating laptop mockup.
- **Rhythm:** clear light/dark or light/tinted alternation every 1–2 sections. Each section has
  a job: what we do → how we do it → proof (real clients, real numbers, real names) → who we
  are → contact.
- **Density:** medium. Text can be longer than in tech-minimal because the voice carries it;
  keep paragraphs to 2–4 lines.
- **Structure devices:** eyebrows written as short phrases, not labels; numbered steps only for
  a real process; a "principles" or "how we work" section with one concrete example per item.
- **Imagery:** photos of the actual team, workspace, product in use; illustration only if it can
  be consistent across the whole site. One real face on the page raises trust more than any
  badge.
- **Type:** a display face with personality (a humanist serif, a rounded or geometric sans with
  distinct letterforms) paired with a plain readable body. The display face is the signature
  candidate.
- **Motion budget:** minimal. Hover on links/buttons; maybe one illustrative micro-interaction
  that explains a feature. CSS only.
- **Nav:** simple, few items, CTA visible; can carry a one-line tagline under the logo.
- **Footer:** warm, with a real address/contact, a short line about the company, useful links.
  Not a dark corporate block by default.

## What ruins this register
Corporate filler copy ("soluciones integrales", "comprometidos con la excelencia"); invented
testimonials; icon grids; illustration mixed from different styles; a hero that could belong
to any company in the rubro.

## Verifier notes
- C5 Copy reads as a specific person/company speaking; no filler phrases
- C6 At least one real image (team, place, product in use) — no stock, no invented faces
